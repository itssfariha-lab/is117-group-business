# Member 3 — Existing Salon Website Review

**Researcher:** Tasnim Fariha  
**Date reviewed:** November 23, 2026  
**Salon:** Beautyychick Studios (Newark, NJ)  
**Original website:** https://beautyychickstudios.glossgenius.com/services

## Observations from the published services page
This is an actual beauty studio's service menu, rather than a generic software provider. The published page includes individual service descriptions, visible prices, estimated durations, and “Select” controls. Examples shown on the page include:
- Classic Lash Full Set: $120, 150 minutes, with a $30 deposit required.
- Brow Wax: $20, 15 minutes.
- Soft Glam makeup: $150, 75 minutes, with a $50 deposit required.
- Some services show starting prices (e.g., $65+) or multiple options instead of a single fixed price.

**Evidence:** Original services page linked above, reviewed November 23, 2026. Prices and availability may change.

## Strengths (based on page content)
1. **Price and time transparency.** Prices and durations appear next to services, helping customers compare options.
2. **Specific service descriptions.** Lash, brow, and makeup descriptions help distinguish treatments.
3. **Deposit disclosure.** The menu labels deposit requirements for certain services before selection.

## Weaknesses / potential usability concerns
1. **Long service list.** The page contains many treatments and options; visitors may need to scroll to locate one category. This is a design concern, not a measured usability failure.
2. **Mixed price formats.** Some entries show fixed amounts while others show “+” starting prices; customers must read carefully to understand whether the displayed price is final.
3. **Booking completion not verified.** The visible service-selection controls do not establish that the full checkout or booking flow works; no appointment was submitted or purchased.

## Design decisions for The Beauty Salon
| Source observation | Proposed design choice | Planned check |
|---|---|---|
| Services have visible prices and durations | Put service name, short description, estimated duration, and clearly labeled sample price together | Can visitors identify prices before starting a request? |
| Deposits are disclosed beside services | Do not imply payments or deposits exist in our class prototype | Does the page avoid misleading payment claims? |
| Extensive service menu | Organize nail, lash, brow, and makeup categories in short scannable groups | Can a visitor find each category quickly? |
| Booking controls are available | Provide an obvious Request Appointment action | Does the link lead to the form when Member 2 integrates it? |
| Booking completion was not verified | Confirmation must state “request received — pending approval” only after a successful save | Does a saved request have a unique ID and pending status? |

## Limits and follow-up
This is a **source-based page review**, not a user study or live booking test. No claims are made about accessibility, mobile usability, payment processing, or successful appointment booking. The group should review the original page independently and record any additional observations before finalizing the design.
